YouTube Automatico

Private workflow automation for managing two YouTube channels: Actually Explained and Buck Got Wrecked.

About the Application

YouTube Automatico is an owner-operated tool that connects Google Drive and YouTube to review video files, detect potential duplicates and manage video uploads and scheduled publication.

The application is intended for use by its owner and authorized Google accounts.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026.

The application requests Google account authorization to access relevant YouTube channel information, upload videos and read designated Google Drive files.

Google OAuth credentials and tokens are used to authenticate requests. The application may process video filenames, video metadata, channel identifiers and publication status to operate the workflow.

Credentials are intended to be stored securely in private automation secrets, rather than published in the application's source code.

The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Authorized users can revoke access through Google Account Connections.

For privacy inquiries or requests concerning application-held data, contact buckandfriends.studion@gmail.com.
